Hello! I know nobody has read all this stuff yet (legit no GitHub watchers). But that's okay, because this
was mainly for myself. I don't want to be some Penn influencer, larper, or blogger. I just want a record of
what I do in my life to look back on fondly. So, that begs the question.....

WHERE IS THE RECORD?

ONE MONTH. THERE IS ONE MONTH LEFT OF FALL SEMESTER.

What have I done?

Certainly not as much as I would like to have. You know I went to an alumni gathering on Saturday for Clark
Scholars, and an upperclassman told me "My only advice is be 'that guy' in whatever field you love. For
example, Massimo loves FPGAs so he should become the FPGA guy." Funny. BECAUSE I HAVE BARELY TOUCHED MY
DE10-Nano. I WANTED TO PLAY CHAOS;HEAD NOAH AS WELL TO PRACTICE JAPANESE. I haven't. ON AND ON.

Time slips from my hands. I feel like Urashima Tarō. I am having so much fun hanging out with friends,
playing games with friends, and chudding around for what feels like a short time, but then I get hit with
that guilt and open the chest realizing all my lost time is GONE. I want to build so much cool stuff. Heck,
that's why I came to college in the first place over trade school. The money doesn't matter to me. I just
dream of being "that guy" and being able to rediscover myself and my passions.

SO SOMETHING HAS TO CHANGE. TODAY, NOW, ETC, WHATEVER TERM YOU WANT TO USE.

I AM STARTING A DAILY LOG. I WILL MAKE ONE POST ON THIS WEBSITE DAILY DETAILING WHAT I DO.

This will be separate from regular posts, which I will try to make one regular/hobby post weekly, so stay
tuned. I will also add all my classwork on the website so I feel semi better about myself.

Thanks for listening to my yap as always. You may have to see it daily now -_-. No. You will see it daily.
