I first started going rock climbing about two and a half months ago at this point, during Penn First Plus.
I was working out at Pottruck one day and I remember seeing someone at the top of the rock wall and I was
like, wow, could I do that too?

![The climbing wall at Pottruck, seen from the upper level](/images/pottruck-rock-wall.jpg)

I went downstairs and immediately signed the waiver. The shoes felt super super tight and the harness was
also tight, but I went on to top climb. I tried an easy 5.6 first and I was just barely able to make it up
to the top with a few breaks along the way. My forearms were absolutely fried that day, and the next day
they felt super sore. I then started going whenever I could, sometimes even 4 times a week. I was bouldering
this time and started with the V0 on the wall and worked my way up to a V2 in half a month.

Then in early August I decided to get my belay certification so I could belay my friends. I wanted them to
experience the rush of adrenaline being that high up gave. So, I signed up for the Climbing 101 class, paid
20 dollars for it :(, and my class was me, a Wharton professor, and a materials science PhD student. For 4
hours we learned how to belay people, how to tie the figure eight knot, how to handle the GriGri, and the
different types of holds on the rock wall. My teacher was really nice and it was a lot of fun. BUT then on
August 5th came test day for the belay certification. It should have been a really easy routine: tie the
figure eight knot, as Liam taught me, strangle the alien and poke it through the eye, then set up the GriGri,
state the calls, and get the pass of greatness. My two classmates did it with ease as expected, and then it
was my turn.

I FAILED!!!!!!!!!!!!!!

You heard me right.... I FAILED! I FORGOT TO POKE THE ALIEN IN THE EYE!!

It was one of the most humbling experiences I have ever had. I felt so stupid seeing my classmates pass first
try easily while I failed and disappointed my instructor. I almost felt like crying knowing I would have to
tell all my friends how I failed despite skipping physics to take the test. To make matters worse, I tore two
callouses on my hand rock climbing that day, putting me out of commission for close to a week.

That day was a reality check for me. No matter how easy anything seemed, if I didn't put effort in I could
mess it up in a heartbeat. I felt so stupid, but not resigned. Even if I couldn't climb, I went to the rock
wall and practiced my knot tying for 3 days straight before my Penn First Plus midterms. I retook the test on
August 11th and THANKFULLY

I PASSED! Isn't my certification so pretty :)

![My Penn Adventure Climbing Wall top rope certification card](/images/belay-certification.jpg)

After this I just continued climbing and dragging all my friends to the rock wall to belay them. I managed to
do a V3 (Dinnerbone) before PFP ended and it felt so surreal. I will never not enjoy the feeling of my
forearms hurting. I also joined Rock Climbing Club at Penn as soon as the year started up. I will continue to
rock climb and provide some updates if I hit any new V grades, so stay tuned for that.

Thanks for listening to my yap. I actually have a white paper due today and Rock Climbing Club at night, so I
have to cut my post here.
